# KYC requirements — corporate client

A complete corporate KYC file contains:

1. Certificate of incorporation or equivalent extract from the register.
2. Articles of association.
3. Identification of every director.
4. **Ownership structure chart** showing every shareholder with 25% or more, up to the natural persons.
5. Source of funds statement.
6. Board resolution authorising the account relationship.

The reviewer, not the assistant, takes the final onboarding decision.
